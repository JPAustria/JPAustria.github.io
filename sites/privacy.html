Privacy Policy

Privacy-first by design.

Finalizer is built as a local macOS audio tool. The app is intended to analyze and play audio files on your Mac without uploading your audio to a cloud service.

1. Controller

Responsible for this website and the Finalizer app is Johannes Puntigam, Austria.

Contact details are provided in the Impressum and support information linked in the footer of this website.

2. Finalizer macOS app

Finalizer is designed for local audio analysis, playback, delivery checks, reports and related mastering workflow tasks on macOS.

The app does not require a user account for local use, does not upload your audio files for analysis, and is not designed to track listening behavior for advertising purposes.

3. Website access data

When you visit this static information website, technical access data may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website and keep it secure. This can include the requested page or file, date and time of access, IP address, browser type, operating system, referrer URL and similar technical log data.

This data is used for technical operation, security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. It is not used by Finalizer for advertising profiling.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website is intended to work without analytics, advertising pixels, newsletter tracking or non-essential cookies.
