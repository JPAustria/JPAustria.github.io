Support & Privacy

Support

For help, bug reports, or feedback about this macOS app,, contact:

asoulguerilla@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

This app processes audio files and project data locally on your Mac.

This app does not collect usage analytics, does not track users, and does not upload audio files, projects, or personal data automatically.

Files are accessed only when selected by the user through macOS file access and sandbox permissions.

Feedback and Bug Reports

If you choose to contact support or send a bug report, you may choose to share information such as your email address, app version, macOS version, Mac model, system language, timestamp, a description of the issue, the last recorded app action, and the last recorded error message.

This information is only sent if you choose to send it. Finalizer does not send diagnostic information automatically.
